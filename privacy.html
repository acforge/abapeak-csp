Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

1. Who We Are

AbaPeak is a web-based tool that helps Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs) and Board Certified Assistant Behavior Analysts (BCaBAs) generate structured ABA session notes in DAP format for CPT codes 97155 (Protocol Modification) and 97156 (Caregiver Training).

AbaPeak is operated by IT Integrated Solutions LLC. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@abapeak.com.

2. This Service Is Not a HIPAA-Covered Entity

AbaPeak is not a HIPAA-covered entity and does not function as a Business Associate under HIPAA. AbaPeak is designed so that client identifiers are replaced with placeholders on your device before any request is transmitted to our servers, so that our servers are not intended to receive Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA. The on-device scrubber runs automatically, but no automated scrubber can be guaranteed to detect every possible identifier; entering real PHI is a misuse of the Service, and you remain responsible for the content you submit.

You remain responsible for how you use AbaPeak in your clinical practice and for ensuring your documentation practices comply with applicable laws, regulations, and your organization's policies.

3. What Information We Collect

a. Email address

If you contact us through the support form, your email address and message are routed through Formspree (see §7) to our inbox. If you purchase a subscription, Lemon Squeezy collects your email at checkout and we use it to communicate trial status, billing, and subscription details.

b. Payment information

Subscription payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which serves as the Merchant of Record. AbaPeak does not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy governs payment data.

c. Usage analytics

AbaPeak does not currently use third-party web analytics or advertising trackers. If we introduce a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics service in the future, we will update this policy to name it before it is enabled.

d. License key

When you enter your license key, it is stored locally on your device and sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our servers only to verify that you hold a valid license through our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy. Our servers do not retain your raw license key; for abuse-prevention logging it is recorded only in one-way hashed form.

e. Trial abuse-prevention data (free trial only)

To keep the free trial fair and prevent circumvention of trial limits, AbaPeak's trial mode processes two pieces of technical metadata:

  • A device fingerprint derived from your browser's user-agent string, kept only for your current browser session and transmitted only as a one-way (SHA-256) hash.
  • Your IP address, used only in hashed form.

These are used solely to enforce daily trial-note limits and prevent abuse. They are not linked to any session note content, client information, or account identity; are never used for advertising or cross-site tracking; and are not collected once you activate a paid license. This metadata is processed at our edge network purely for rate-limiting, under the same no-identifiable-PHI-logging posture as the rest of the service.

4. How AbaPeak Handles Session Data

AbaPeak's architecture is designed to minimize the clinical information that leaves your device:

  • You enter session information using a client alias (e.g., "Client-07"), not a real client name or identifier.
  • Before any request is transmitted, a built-in scrubber runs entirely on your device. It identifies and replaces any detected client identifiers with placeholders.
  • What is transmitted to generate a note carries placeholders in place of the identifiers the scrubber detected. Our servers and our AI provider receive only that scrubbed, placeholder-containing content — not your original input. Because no automated scrubber can be guaranteed to detect every identifier, you remain responsible for using client aliases.
  • Generated notes are not stored on our servers. Notes are saved only in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your own device.

5. What We Do Not Collect

AbaPeak is designed not to receive or store:

  • Real client names, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • ICD-10 codes, diagnostic information, clinical notes, or session content in readable form
  • Any Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under HIPAA
  • Behavioral session recordings (screen replays, heatmaps, or similar tracking tools)

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold about you, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS), one-way hashing of license keys in our server logs, and edge-infrastructure security via Cloudflare. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

7. Third-Party Services

AbaPeak uses the following third-party services:

  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing and license management (Merchant of Record). Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — web hosting, edge network, and DNS. Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — bot protection on our forms. Runs in your browser when you load the page and may process technical signals (such as your IP address and browser characteristics) to tell people from automated traffic. Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — AI model provider for note generation. Receives only the scrubbed, placeholder-containing request from your device — not your original input. Privacy Policy
  • Formspree — contact form submission routing. When you contact us via our support form, your email address and message are transmitted through Formspree to our inbox. Privacy Policy

8. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you have an active subscription. You may request deletion at any time by emailing hello@abapeak.com and we will respond within 30 days. Generated notes and session data are stored only on your device and can be cleared from within the app at any time.
